The best part of the evening is the moment the house goes quiet. The kettle clicks off. The last window turns blue. For a little while, there is nothing to catch up with.[^quiet]

I’ve been trying to leave more room for those moments. Fewer tabs open, a notebook within reach, and a walk with no particular destination.

> Pay attention to what you would otherwise walk past.

[^quiet]: Quiet is relative. There is still a rather opinionated blackbird outside the window.
